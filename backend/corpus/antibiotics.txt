Title: Antibiotic Use and Resistance – WHO Guidance
Source: World Health Organization (WHO)

Antibiotics treat bacterial infections. They do not work against viruses such as the common cold, flu, or most sore throats.

Key facts:
- Taking antibiotics when not needed contributes to antibiotic resistance – a major global health threat.
- Always finish the full prescribed course unless a doctor advises otherwise.
- Never share leftover antibiotics or use someone else’s prescription.
- Viral infections usually resolve with rest, fluids, and symptom relief.
