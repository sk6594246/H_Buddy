Title: Common Health Myths – Evidence Snapshot
Source: Compiled from WHO, CDC, and established medical consensus

Several widely shared claims are not supported by strong evidence.

Key facts:
- Cracking knuckles has not been shown to cause arthritis in well-designed studies.
- You do not need to wait 30–60 minutes after eating before swimming; the risk of cramps is low for most people.
- Sugar does not “feed cancer” in the simplistic way often claimed; all cells use glucose, and starving the body of carbohydrate is not a proven cancer treatment.
- Detox teas and extreme cleanses lack robust evidence for removing “toxins” beyond what the liver and kidneys already do.
- Cold weather itself does not cause colds; viruses do. Dry indoor air and closer contact in winter help viruses spread.
