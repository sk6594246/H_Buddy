Title: Physical Activity – WHO Guidelines
Source: World Health Organization (WHO)

Regular physical activity benefits almost everyone.

Key facts:
- Adults should aim for at least 150–300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity per week, or 75–150 minutes of vigorous activity, plus muscle-strengthening on 2 or more days.
- “No pain, no gain” is a myth – moderate activity already brings large health benefits.
- Sitting for long periods is associated with higher health risks; breaking up sitting time helps.
- Exercise is beneficial for mental health and can reduce symptoms of mild-to-moderate depression and anxiety.

Trusted references:
