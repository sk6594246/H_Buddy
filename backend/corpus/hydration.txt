Title: Water and Hydration – Basic Consensus
Source: World Health Organization (WHO), general medical consensus

Adequate fluid intake supports normal body function. Needs vary by climate, activity, and individual factors.

Key facts:
- For most healthy people, thirst is a good guide. The old “8 glasses a day” rule is a rough average, not a strict requirement.
- Plain water is the best everyday drink. Sugary drinks add calories without much nutritional value.
- Over-hydration (drinking extreme amounts of water in a short time) is rare but can be dangerous.
- In hot weather or during intense exercise, extra fluids and electrolytes may be needed.
