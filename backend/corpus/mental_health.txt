Title: Mental Health Basics – WHO
Source: World Health Organization (WHO)

Mental health is an integral part of overall health. Stigma and misinformation can prevent people from seeking help.

Key facts:
- Depression and anxiety are common and treatable. Effective treatments include talking therapies, medication when appropriate, and lifestyle support.
- Social media use can have both positive and negative effects; excessive use, especially late at night, is associated with poorer sleep and mood in some studies.
- “Just think positive” is not an adequate treatment for clinical depression or anxiety disorders.
- Seeking professional help is a sign of strength, not weakness.
