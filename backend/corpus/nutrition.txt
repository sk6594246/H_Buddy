Title: Basic Nutrition Principles – WHO & NHS
Source: World Health Organization (WHO), National Health Service (NHS)

A balanced diet supports overall health. No single food or supplement is a miracle cure.

Key facts:
- Fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean proteins, and healthy fats form the foundation of a healthy diet.
- Extreme detox diets, juice cleanses, or “alkaline” diets lack strong scientific support for curing disease.
- Vitamin and mineral supplements can help when there is a diagnosed deficiency, but they are not a substitute for a varied diet for most healthy people.
- Sugar-sweetened beverages and ultra-processed foods are linked to higher risk of obesity, type 2 diabetes, and heart disease when consumed in excess.
