Title: Sleep and Health – WHO & CDC Guidance
Source: World Health Organization (WHO), Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)

Sleep is essential for physical and mental health. Most adults need 7–9 hours of quality sleep per night.

Key facts:
- Chronic short sleep is linked to higher risk of heart disease, diabetes, obesity, and depression.
- “Catching up” on weekends does not fully reverse the effects of weekday sleep debt.
- Screens (phones, tablets, computers) emit blue light that can delay melatonin release and make it harder to fall asleep.
- A consistent sleep schedule, dark cool room, and limiting caffeine after midday support better sleep.
